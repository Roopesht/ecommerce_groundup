# Module Requirements: Marketing (Core Marketplace Platform)

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Module:** Marketing (Core Platform)  
**Date:** 2026-05-04  

---

## 1. Product Context

### The Problem
Organic produce market is fragmented and trust-constrained. Farmers lose significant margin (30–50%) to wholesalers and intermediaries, reducing their incentive to produce organic. Customers want authentic organic produce but cannot verify farmer credentials, production methods, or origin — they lack transparency and trust. This creates a barrier: customers are willing to pay premium prices for organic, but they don't know whom to trust.

### The Value Proposition
A direct-to-consumer marketplace that removes intermediaries and provides transparency. Customers can see exactly which farmer grew their produce, understand farming practices, and contact farmers directly to ask questions about production methods, lead times, and quality. Farmers capture full margin, build direct relationships with customers, and are known by name and reputation.

### Primary Users (Product Level)
- **Farmers:** Organic produce growers selling through intermediaries, seeking direct access to premium-price customers
- **Customers:** Bangalore and Cochin residents with disposable income who actively seek organic produce and value authenticity and origin transparency

## 2. User Personas

### Persona: Farmer

**Who they are**  
An organic produce grower who currently sells through middlemen, wholesalers, or local markets and loses significant margin in the process. They have invested in sustainable, organic practices but are invisible to customers who would value and pay premium prices for their produce.

**What they want**
- A direct channel to reach customers who value organic and will pay premium prices
- To be known by name and have their farming practices visible
- To capture the full margin from their produce instead of losing 30–50% to intermediaries
- To build direct relationships with customers and prove the authenticity of their organic practices

**What they fear**
- Customers continuing to buy from unverified sources or competing platforms that don't highlight farmer credentials
- Being invisible — having no way to prove the authenticity of their organic practices
- Losing customers to farms that successfully market themselves and build direct relationships

**How they use this module**
- Create and maintain a farmer profile with details about their location, farming practices, certifications, and story
- List their produce with harvest dates, lead times, pricing, and production method details
- Receive inquiries from customers asking about production methods, availability, and quality
- Respond directly to customer questions to build trust and close sales
- See order details, manage availability, and coordinate with customers on delivery timing
- Monitor customer reviews and ratings to build their reputation over time

---

### Persona: Customer

**Who they are**  
A person in Bangalore or Cochin with disposable income who actively seeks organic produce and values knowing the origin and story behind what they eat. They are willing to pay premium prices for verified authenticity and direct accountability.

**What they want**
- Authentic organic produce with full transparency: knowing the exact farmer who grew it
- Understanding of farming practices and production methods so they can verify claims
- Ability to contact farmers directly with questions before purchasing
- Assurance that their premium price goes to real quality and sustainability, not just marketing
- Confidence in their purchasing decision based on farmer reputation and reviews

**What they fear**
- Buying mislabeled or low-quality produce claiming to be "organic" without verification
- Wasting money on products that don't meet quality expectations
- Discovering after purchase that the farmer's practices don't align with their values
- Platforms that hide farmer identity or provide no way to verify authenticity
- Being deceived by greenwashing or unsubstantiated organic claims

**How they use this module**
- Browse farmer profiles to learn about farming practices and location
- Search or filter produce by farmer name, location, farming method, or produce type
- Read detailed produce listings that include origin, lead time, and farmer information
- Contact farmers directly with questions about production methods, availability, or quality
- Review farmer profiles and customer reviews to make informed purchasing decisions
- Add produce to cart, complete checkout, and pay securely
- Track order status and delivery timing
- Provide reviews and ratings after receiving orders to build transparency for other customers

---

## 3. Module Objective

This module is the direct-to-consumer marketplace that solves the transparency and trust gap in organic produce. It enables farmers to list produce with full context (origin, lead time, farmer credentials) and customers to discover, verify, and purchase directly from farms. By removing intermediaries, the module reduces prices for customers while enabling farmers to capture full value, building a sustainable two-sided marketplace.

**Scope:** This module handles everything required for farmers to list produce, customers to discover and purchase, and both parties to communicate directly. It includes end-to-end transactional capability: browsing, search, farmer profile access, direct messaging, cart management, checkout, payment, order confirmation, and order tracking.

---

## 4. Success Outcomes

These measurable results define module success:

- **User Acquisition:** 1,000 registered users (farmers + customers combined) acquired within 90 days of launch
- **Engagement:** At least 50% of registered users are active on the platform at least once per week
- **Revenue Traction:** Average order value (AOV) reaches ₹500 or higher, demonstrating customer willingness to pay premium and order volume sufficient to sustain farmer income

**Why these matter**  
User acquisition proves market demand and product-market fit. Engagement proves users find value and return regularly. AOV proves farmers can capture sufficient margin and customers willingly pay premium for direct-from-farm produce. Together, these outcomes validate that the product solves the trust and transparency problem and is sustainable for both user groups.

## 5. Must-Have Features

Features the module cannot launch without. These are user-centric (what users can do, not how):

### For Farmers

- **Create and manage farmer profile**
  - Enter location (village/city in Bangalore or Cochin)
  - Describe farming practices (organic methods, certifications, story)
  - Upload profile photo or media
  - Edit profile at any time

- **List produce with complete details**
  - Specify produce type, quantity, and harvest date
  - Set price
  - Describe production method (e.g., no pesticides, hand-picked, seasonal)
  - Specify lead time (how long before delivery after order)
  - Upload photos
  - Mark availability status (in stock, out of stock, limited)
  - Edit or remove listings

- **Manage orders**
  - View incoming customer orders
  - See order details (quantity, delivery location, customer contact)
  - Confirm order receipt and coordinate delivery with customer
  - Update order status (confirmed, preparing, ready for pickup/delivery)

- **Respond to customer inquiries**
  - Receive direct messages from customers asking about production methods, availability, or quality
  - Respond with detailed information
  - View conversation history with each customer

- **Build reputation**
  - View customer reviews and ratings on their profile
  - See aggregate farmer rating
  - Understand what customers value (via reviews)

---

### For Customers

- **Discover produce and farmers**
  - Browse farmer profiles to learn about their location, practices, and story
  - Search produce by farmer name, location, or produce type
  - Filter by produce availability and lead time
  - View farmer profile including reviews and ratings

- **Make informed purchasing decisions**
  - Access detailed produce listings with origin, harvest date, and production method
  - Contact farmers directly with questions about production methods, quality, lead time, or availability
  - Read customer reviews and ratings for farmers
  - View photos and descriptions that help assess quality

- **Purchase and track**
  - Add produce to cart
  - Proceed to checkout
  - Provide delivery address and contact information
  - Complete payment (secure payment processing)
  - Receive order confirmation
  - Track order status from confirmed → preparing → ready for delivery
  - See estimated delivery date/window

- **Provide feedback**
  - Leave review and rating after order delivery
  - Help other customers make informed decisions
  - Build farmer reputation

## 7. Out of Scope

This module explicitly does **NOT** do:

- **Logistics and delivery operations**  
  The module does not perform delivery, pickup coordination, or last-mile logistics. Third-party logistics providers handle fulfillment. The module integrates with third-party logistics tracking; farmers and customers coordinate delivery timing directly through the platform.

- **Produce quality certification or guarantee**  
  The module does not guarantee, certify, or test produce quality. The platform surfaces farmer information, produce details, and customer reviews. Customers decide what to purchase based on available information. Quality assurance, testing, or audits are not the platform's responsibility.

- **Farmer certification or compliance vetting**  
  The module does not verify farmer organic credentials or audit farming practices. Farmers self-report their farming methods and certifications. The platform does not validate claims or perform compliance checks.

- **Supply chain audits or traceability at scale**  
  While the module provides transparency (farmer name, location, practices, reviews), it does not perform backend auditing of farms or production methods. Full supply chain traceability and audit trails are not in scope.

- **Geographic expansion beyond Bangalore and Cochin**  
  The module launches in Bangalore and Cochin only, where delivery infrastructure is established. Expansion to other regions is blocked until initial delivery lines are proven repeatable and sustainable.

- **Subscription or loyalty systems** (Phase 1)  
  Phase 1 focuses on transactional orders. Subscriptions, loyalty tiers, or recurring order automation are future modules.

- **Payments and invoicing for farmers**  
  The module does not handle farmer accounting, invoices, or tax documentation. Payment processing is integrated; farmer payout workflows and financial reporting are not included.

## 9. Design Principles

Developers should keep these principles in mind:

1. **Transparency first:** All farmer information, produce details, and reviews should be easily accessible; the module exists to make information visible.

2. **Direct communication:** Farmers and customers should be able to contact each other directly through the platform; avoid forcing interaction through the platform for follow-up (i.e., don't lock communication behind the module).

3. **Trust through reviews and reputation:** Without quality certification or platform auditing, farmer reviews and customer feedback are the primary trust signals. Make these visible and easy to find.

4. **Farmer and customer empowerment:** Both user groups should feel they have agency. Farmers control what they list and how they describe it. Customers have full information to make informed decisions.

5. **Simplicity over features:** Launch with core capabilities (list, browse, contact, purchase, track). Do not add complexity unless it directly supports one of the three success outcomes.

- **Data residency:** Does customer or farmer data have to be stored in India specifically?
- **Third-party logistics partners:** Which logistics providers are confirmed as integration partners?
- **Customer verification:** Is there a KYC (Know Your Customer) requirement for customer accounts?
- **Pricing and commission:** Does the platform take a commission from orders? If so, what percentage and how is it disclosed to farmers?
