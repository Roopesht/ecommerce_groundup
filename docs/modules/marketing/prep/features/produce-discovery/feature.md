# Feature: Produce Discovery

## Summary
Customers can browse and search produce listings across all farmers. They can filter by produce type, availability, lead time, and other attributes. This feature helps customers find specific items they need and compare options across different farmers.

## User Story
As a customer, I want to browse and search produce listings by type, farmer, location, and availability so that I can find the specific produce I want to buy.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Customer can navigate to a "Browse Produce" or "Shop" section
- Customer can view a grid or list of produce listings with: produce photo, produce type, farmer name, price, availability status, lead time, and farmer rating
- Customer can search produce by type (e.g., tomatoes, spinach)
- Customer can search produce by farmer name
- Customer can filter by produce availability status (in stock, limited, out of stock)
- Customer can filter by lead time range
- Customer can filter by location (Bangalore or Cochin)
- Customer can sort results by price, rating, or recency
- Customer can click on a produce listing to view detailed information
- Customer can see on the detail page: produce type, quantity available, harvest date, price, production method, lead time, photos, farmer name, farmer profile link, and farmer reviews
- Customer can add produce to cart directly from the detail page
- Customer can view multiple photos of the produce
- Customer can see which farmer grows each produce item
- Customer can navigate back to browse other produce

## Priority
High — This is a must-have feature; without it, customers cannot find and browse specific items to purchase.

## Dependencies
user-registration, user-login, produce-listing-management, farmer-profile-management
