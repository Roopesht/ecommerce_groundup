# Modules Overview

## Module 1
Marketing 

### What It Does
List the products on the platform, and when customer clicks on that product, they will have the choices  about farmers and the produce. 
Farmers can list their produce on the platform, and customers can browse and order the produce online. The platform will provide transparency and trust to the customers by providing information about the farmers and the produce.
Cart, checkout, payment, order tracking, delivery tracking, and customer support.

### Why Now
There is lot of awareness about the benefits of organic produce, and people are willing to pay premium for it. Right now there are trust issues with the organic produce, and people are not sure about the quality of the produce they are buying. This platform will provide the transparency and trust that customers are looking for when buying organic produce online.

### Non-Goals
It will not do the logistics and delivery, it will be done by the third-party logistics providers. 
This will not guarantee any quality of the produce, it will provide the information about the farmers and the produce, and the customers can make their own decision about the quality of the produce.
