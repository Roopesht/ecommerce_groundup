# Product Vision
Client has a organic produce, and they want to expand their business by providing a platform for the customers to order the organic produce online.  This will helpp the customers to get the exact place where the produce is coming from, and they can also get the information about the farmers who are growing the produce. since its organic produce customers are willing to order in advance (the lead time will be provided by the farmers) 

## Problem Statement
Farmers need a platform to sell their organic produce directly to the customers, and customers need a platform to order organic produce online. The current market is fragmented, and there is no single platform that connects farmers and customers directly. This leads to inefficiencies in the supply chain and higher prices for the customers.
The user has the right to know where the produce is coming from
## Target Audience
This website is available to people in Bangalore and Cochin where the current delivery lines are steup. As every has access to the internet and smartphone, the target audience is anyone who is interested in buying organic produce online. 
People who can pay premium


## Market Opportunity
People are ready to pay premium for organic produce. The organic food market is growing rapidly, and there is a huge demand for organic produce. 
The farmers also need the premium for their organic produce, which is costly to produce. 

## Why Now
There is lot of awareness about the benefits of organic produce, and people are willing to pay premium for it. Right now there are trust issues with the organic produce, and people are not sure about the quality of the produce they are buying. This platform will provide the transparency and trust that customers are looking for when buying organic produce online.

## Competitive Landscape
There are some organic platforms in the market, but they are not providing the transparency and trust that customers are looking for.
