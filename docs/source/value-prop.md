# Core Value Proposition

## What We Do
Provide the platform on web and mobile (responsive website) for the customers to order organic produce online directly from the farmers. 
Farmers can list their produce on the platform, and customers can browse and order the produce online. The platform will provide transparency and trust to the customers by providing information about the farmers and the produce.

## What Makes Us Different
The platform will provide transparency and trust and location information to the customers by providing information about the farmers and the produce. 

## Key Differentiators
For every produce they can contact the farmers to know specifics, which will improve the trust and transparency.
