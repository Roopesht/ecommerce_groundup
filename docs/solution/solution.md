# Solution: Organic Produce Direct-to-Consumer Platform

## Product Vision

Farmers need a direct channel to sell organic produce to conscious consumers. Today, the market is fragmented—there is no single platform connecting farmers and customers. This creates inefficiencies in the supply chain and inflates prices for customers, while farmers lose margin to intermediaries. Customers who want organic produce lack transparency: they cannot verify farmer credentials, production methods, or produce origin, which creates trust barriers in a market where authenticity is the primary value driver.

## Why Now

Awareness of organic produce benefits has grown significantly, and consumers are willing to pay premium prices. However, a critical gap exists: trust. Customers are uncertain about the quality and authenticity of organic produce they buy. This platform solves that gap by providing direct access to farmer information and produce details, enabling customers to make informed decisions and farmers to capture full value from their premium products.

## Core Value Proposition

Unlike existing organic platforms, this product provides **direct transparency**: customers can see exactly which farmer grew their produce, access detailed farmer and produce information, and contact farmers directly to ask specifics about production methods, lead times, and quality. This direct farmer-customer connection replaces intermediaries, reduces prices, and builds trust through direct communication and location-based visibility.

## Success Definition

The product succeeds when:

- **User Acquisition:** 1,000 registered users acquired within 90 days of launch
- **Engagement:** At least 50% of users are active on the platform at least once per week
- **Revenue Traction:** Average order value (AOV) reaches ₹500+

## Module List

**Core Platform** (`core-marketplace`)
- **What it does:** Enable farmers to list produce with details (origin, lead time, farmer info) and customers to browse, contact farmers, add items to cart, checkout, pay, and track orders.
- **Why now:** This is the minimal viable product to solve the transparency and direct-connection problem. It launches in Bangalore and Cochin where delivery infrastructure is established. Customer demand for organic with direct-farmer access cannot be served until this platform exists.
- **Non-goals:**
  - Platform does not operate logistics or delivery; third-party providers handle fulfillment.
  - Platform does not guarantee or certify produce quality; it surfaces farmer information and customer reviews, and customers decide based on available information.
  - Not in scope: farmer certification programs, quality assurance testing, or supply chain audits.
  - Not in scope: expansion beyond Bangalore and Cochin until initial delivery lines are proven.
