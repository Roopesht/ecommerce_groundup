# Solution: User Personas

## Persona: Farmer
**Who they are:** An organic produce grower who currently sells through middlemen, wholesalers, or local markets and loses significant margin in the process.

**What they want:** A direct channel to reach customers who value and will pay premium prices for organic produce. They want to be known by name, have their farming practices visible, and capture the full margin rather than lose it to intermediaries.

**What they fear:** Customers continuing to buy from unverified sources or competing platforms that don't highlight farmer credentials and production methods. They fear being invisible—having no way to build direct relationships with customers or prove the authenticity of their organic practices.

**How they relate:** Customers browse their profile and produce listings, and can contact them directly with questions about production methods, lead times, and specifics. Farmers depend on customers trusting their information and reputation to generate orders.

---

## Persona: Customer
**Who they are:** A person in Bangalore or Cochin with disposable income who actively seeks organic produce and values knowing the origin and story behind what they eat.

**What they want:** Authentic organic produce with full transparency: knowing the exact farmer who grew it, understanding their farming practices, and being able to contact them with questions. They want assurance that their premium price goes toward real quality and sustainability, not just marketing.

**What they fear:** Buying mislabeled or low-quality produce claiming to be "organic" without verification. They fear wasting money on products that don't meet their quality expectations or discover the farmer's practices don't align with their values. They distrust platforms that hide farmer identity or provide no way to verify authenticity.

**How they relate:** Customers depend on farmers to provide accurate information, maintain quality, and respond to inquiries. They interact with farmers directly through the platform to clarify details before purchasing.
